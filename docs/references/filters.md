# Filters — the selling point

ApplyDiff's filter system is a headline feature: it is the difference between
"another job board" and "a board that only shows what you would actually take".
Every filter is a first-class, named, saved, and explained.

## Mechanics

- **AND across categories, OR within a category** (Region = SEA *or* EU; AND Remote).
- **Hard vs soft**: a hard filter hides (or marks skipped); a soft filter boosts rank.
- **Explain every decision**: each job shows "matched" / "skipped: no visa sponsorship"
  so nothing is silently dropped.
- **Saved presets**: name a combination ("Remote SEA/EU backend, 4-day week, > $80k")
  and switch it in one click. Presets are shareable as a text block.
- **Applied at scan and in the feed**, so a scan never stores noise you will never read.

---

## 1. Work arrangement
- remote (fully)
- hybrid, with days on-site: 1 / 2 / 3 / 4
- on-site
- **4-day week** / 4.5-day / compressed week
- async-first / flexible hours
- **timezone overlap required** — hours, e.g. "≥ 4h with EST"

## 2. Region & geography
- blocs: APAC/APJ, EMEA, NA, LATAM, Global/Worldwide
- countries: SEA (ID/SG/MY/TH/VN/PH), EU, UK, US, CA, AU/NZ, IN, MENA, LATAM
- "anywhere"/"worldwide" only
- exclude regions

## 3. Employment type
- full-time, part-time, contract, freelance, internship, contract-to-hire
- exclude: commission-only, unpaid

## 4. Seniority
- intern, junior, mid, senior, staff, principal, lead, manager, director
- min level / max level / exclude levels

## 5. Compensation
- min annual (currency: USD/EUR/IDR/SGD/…)
- min hourly / monthly
- equity offered
- salary-disclosed only
- exclude: no salary listed

## 6. Company
- stage: seed, Series A/B/C/D+, public, bootstrapped
- size: 1-10, 11-50, 51-200, 201-1000, 1000+
- industry
- **exclude companies** (blocklist)
- **exclude agencies / staffing / recruiters**
- company verified (legitimacy)

## 7. Visa & logistics
- visa sponsorship offered
- no sponsorship needed (local roles)
- relocation offered
- **contractor-friendly / EOR available** (essential for hiring-from-Indonesia)

## 8. Role & stack
- archetype: backend, frontend, fullstack, mobile, platform, data, ML/AI,
  devops/SRE, security, QA
- must-have keywords (AND)
- nice-to-have keywords (OR)
- exclude keywords
- title match (regex)

## 9. Posting quality & freshness
- posted within: 24h / 3d / 7d / 14d / any
- new since last scan
- **official ATS URL only** (skip aggregator-only links)
- salary disclosed
- **apply-within-24h** flag (recency)

## 10. Application effort
- one-click / easy-apply available
- no cover letter required
- no take-home
- no whiteboard / live-coding
- accepts a PDF attachment

## 11. Benefits (optional, nice-to-have)
- 4-day week
- visa sponsorship
- equity
- learning budget
- health insurance
- minimum paid time off

## Why it sells

Most remote boards give one switch: "remote". ApplyDiff gives the axis a real
candidate actually filters on: arrangement *and* region *and* overlap *and* visa
*and* comp *and* effort. And it explains every skip, so the feed is trusted.
